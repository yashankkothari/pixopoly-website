The game you know

Roll. Buy. Build. Charge your best friend $950 for one night in Toronto.

Forty tiles, two dice, and a bank that never loses its temper. If you have ever flipped a board in anger, you already know how to play.

Every property is a real city, drawn in pixels: the pyramids at sunset in Cairo, the Blue Mosque at dusk in Istanbul, Tokyo Tower under Fuji. Open a deed and the city comes alive.

  • 2 to 6 players
  • 20 to 60 minutes
  • 22 cities
  • CPUs that trade back

Until someone plays a card

Twelve sneaky cards. One wild event every round.

Swap places with the leader. Dodge a hotel bill. Then the round opens with an Earthquake and all your plans are worth exactly nothing.

Both are switches. Turn them off and it's the classic.
